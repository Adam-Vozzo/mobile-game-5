Make the Cat Dizzy!

Pokémon Ranger style — but with a cat and a laser pointer

  1. Move your laser pointer (mouse or finger) to attract the cat.
  2. Draw closed loops around the cat — like circling it with a lasso.
  3. Each loop adds dizziness. Fill the bar before it drains!
  4. Bigger loops = bigger dizziness boost. Faster cats in higher levels.
